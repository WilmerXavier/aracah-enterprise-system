# Case Study: ARACAH Enterprise Management System

Version 1.0 - Status: Draft validated with confirmed information

## 1. Executive Summary

Collaborative development of an enterprise management system for a furniture-sector company, carried out as an applied academic project at the National Autonomous University of Honduras (UNAH).

The project aimed to contribute to the digitalization of operational processes through a web application oriented to business information management.

My participation focused primarily on the development of the Inventory Management module, while also collaborating on other functionalities of the system as part of a development team.
